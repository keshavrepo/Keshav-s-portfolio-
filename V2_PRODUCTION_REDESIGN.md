## SCENE 1 — THE OPENING

_Locked chain: pulse → claim line one → claim line two → reactivity play → click → dive. Seconds unchanged._

1. **Visual Concept — "The void theater."** Dead-center ceremony column: a single signal dot with breathing halo (no neuron drawing). The two locked claim lines staged as exhibition type — 80% of the pixels are type. Identity mark top-left margin rail, caption size. Nothing else renders.
2. **Motion Concept.** The Pulse (pure CSS, 900ms) beats first, one full beat before any word. Claim lines Mask-rise from baseline, visitor-paced. Presence leans the halo <100ms. Click fires **The Iris**: a clip-path aperture expands from the dot, 2000ms dive envelope, decelerating expo; the claim's afterimage dims through it. Reduced twin: instant composed void + crossfade.
3. **Typography.** Statement voice, display-xl, one breath per line; line breaks authored exactly on the locked copy. No furniture type on stage.
4. **Color.** First Light — warm void `#0E0C0A`, paper ink `#F4EDE4`, Pulse Ember `#FF7A3D` (glow) / `#FF9A66` (text tier). No gradient; void purity.
5. **Scroll Experience.** None before entry — one act owns the stage; the runway waits armed behind the Iris.
6. **Transition.** Iris → arrival chamber: composition settles into a bounded spread, 3–4s Breath before the mind resolves.
7. **Interaction.** Hover/focus warms the halo (glow-tier ration); press = dive; Enter/Space identical (S5); Express lamp lit at ~6s on the lower-third rail, never blinking.
8. **Why this is memorable.** A portfolio that opens like a held breath: two sentences alone in warm dark, a heartbeat that notices you, and a door made of light you draw around yourself. The first frame is the retell-unit.

## SCENE 2 — THE MINDSCAPE

_Locked chain: vista (1600ms) → legend (3 items) → whispers → map resolve → Map trigger. Content anchors `mm:_` unchanged.\*

1. **Visual Concept — "The dusk inside."** Nine whisper-lines floating at authored offsets in deep air — thoughts reading like a constellation. Hairline drawn threads connect true inference pairs (truth-binding: no thread without its sentence). At pullback the scatter resolves into the grammar map; the visitor's own route visibly becomes the constellation.
2. **Motion Concept.** Entrance: one **Resolve** (scattered whispers drift into legibility, blur→sharp) then Vista hold. Whispers Breathe (≤8px Murmur, ≤3 concurrent). Threads **Draw** on first contact with each node. Pullback: Cascade into map — thoughts settle onto their verb orbits.
3. **Typography.** Whisper lines in the Analyst voice, small and warm, felt whole at a glance (S75). Legend as three micro-labels on the margin rail: node = a thought · thread = a because · touch = hear it.
4. **Color.** The Dusk Inside — midnight iris `#171432`, ink `#ECE9F6`, Idea Violet `#8B7CF6` (glow) / `#A99CFF` (text). Whisper gradient iris→plum `#241E4A`. No daylight whites.
5. **Scroll Experience.** `p` scrubs the vista-to-map drift: the field slowly organizes as you travel; scrolling up un-resolves honestly.
6. **Transition.** In: Iris lands in the chamber, then the field resolves. Out: **Draw-Across** — the thread continues off-canvas into Grammar's rail; the palette begins migrating toward paper.
7. **Interaction.** Hover = node halo + earned label (whisper of its inference); press = ThoughtCard carrying the full thought; 44px targets, focus parity, two-beat cooldown keeps one voice speaking.
8. **Why this is memorable.** You read a person's thinking as stars, touch a star, and it explains itself in one true sentence — then the sky quietly proves it was a map all along.

## SCENE 4 — THE UNIVERSE

_Locked chain: vista → reveal (1400ms) → 11 entities, 3 tiers → 15 cause→effect threads → heart = Customers → waves via ripple clock. Anchors `un:_` unchanged.\*

1. **Visual Concept — "The brass archive."** The eleven entities are exhibits on a deep archive wall: name, role, altitude — set like engraved labels. CUSTOMERS sits at compositional center with a brass halo (the heart, `HEART_ID`, one hue constant across the journey). Fifteen hairline drawn threads carry verbs; glints are brass dot-pulses, ≤3 at a time.
2. **Motion Concept.** Reveal: 1400ms Cascade, tier by tier (heart → facing → backbone), each exhibit arriving with the Resolve. Consequence waves are **thread pulses traveling the drawn lines** (BFS order stays, shared ripple clock stays — DOM and light agree). Honored numerals land whole; no count-ups. Glint Murmur keeps the wall alive without performing.
3. **Typography.** Exhibit system: Statement headline for the name, Analyst micro for role/stake, tabular numerals staged brass at verdict moments. Labels are furniture-calm until earned — `invited` law restyled, not re-roled.
4. **Color.** The Brass Archive — deep marine `#0E1B2A`, ink `#EDF2F7`, Archive Brass `#E0B24A` (glow) / `#E6BC5E` (text). Marine→ink-slate gradient. Weathers (gravitas/velocity/human) tint ≤20% within the family.
5. **Scroll Experience.** `u` scrubs attention through the tiers: heart first, facing second, backbone last — the wall organizes under your travel rather than paging.
6. **Transition.** In: Migration completes paper→marine. Out: the heartbeat dims to 25%, backbone fades, Migration begins marine→bone as the engine's cliff approaches.
7. **Interaction.** Hover = exhibit label + thread ends light up (one radial relationship, no chains); press = ThoughtCard with the exhibit's full evidence; waves from hover travel the same threads as story waves — one system.
8. **Why this is memorable.** A functioning business rendered as an archive that breathes like it's alive — and when you touch one plate, the reasons travel along engraved lines to the customer at its heart.

## SCENE 5 — THE DECISION ENGINE

_Locked chain: vista → scenario "The Renewal Cliff" (churn 4%→7%, 6 weeks) → 4 evidence → 3 paths (shield/bet/triage) → commit + 1750ms hold → staged consequences 1.2/3.6/6.4/9.6s → reflection. Anchors `de:_`, lock `w` unchanged.\*

1. **Visual Concept — "The red pen."** A bone-quiet bench. Left: the evidence column (four exhibits, equal height). Right: the three paths, set as three columns of identical dignity — no hero, no tilt, no recommended answer. A crimson thread waits undrawn between them. Scenario title as an exhibit hero: the cliff named plainly.
2. **Motion Concept.** Evidence Cascades in at 1400ms. Path hover Draws a crimson underline. Press freezes the world — **total visual stillness for 1750ms** (nothing scrubs, nothing breathes, pulse at 25%) — then the verdict stroke: the crimson thread **Draws** from evidence to chosen path. Consequences Cascade at the locked 1.2/3.6/6.4/9.6s marks as wave-ripples across the archive behind the bench.
3. **Typography.** The two prices of every path set as balanced columns — acceptance and unknown in identical weight. Numerals tabular, ink at rest, crimson only at the commit. The reflection line in Statement, quiet.
4. **Color.** The Red Pen — bone `#F7F4EF`, ink `#141110`, Verdict Crimson `#D13B3B` (glow) / `#B02A2E` (text), rationed ≤3% of any view. No gradients; a cliff has no weather.
5. **Scroll Experience.** `w` holds the world in suspension until commit; at `ENGINE_LOCK_W 0.92` the runway meets a settle-back. After commit, scroll releases into the staged aftermath.
6. **Transition.** In: Migration completes marine→bone; the world's noise falls away. Out: **Dissolve-to-Light** — bone warms toward amber as consequences begin settling.
7. **Interaction.** Hover a path = consequence whispers (both prices) preview; press = commit, irreversible, idempotent on double-tap; Enter/Space identical; the dialog carries the path's full reasoning.
8. **Why this is memorable.** Three answers side by side with nobody winking which is right; one red line; and the whole screen stops breathing for two seconds while you own it. "There was no perfect answer."

## SCENE 6 — THE IMPACT ENGINE

_Locked chain: aftermath of the committed path → per-path settle (affected-first) → lessons → reflection hold (4200ms) → calm law. Anchors `im:_` unchanged.\*

1. **Visual Concept — "The morning verdict."** The archive re-composes into a settled ledger: exhibits glide into calm alignment (the FINAL_LAYOUT + per-path deltas become grid positions warmed from within), numerals realign tabular, lessons annotate the margins as drawn-stroke marginalia. The field itself warms bone→amber-dawn as the settle completes.
2. **Motion Concept.** **The Settle** — the chapter's signature: affected exhibits glide first, staggered, to their calm targets; structure follows; glints dim into order. Calm law runs together: Murmur thins, fog lifts, lightness rises. Honored numerals land whole in marigold at their verdict moments. Reflection holds 4200ms with nothing entering beneath it.
3. **Typography.** Numerals as the monument — display verdicts, tabular, Statement voice. Lessons in the marginalia register: small Analyst lines under a drawn underline. Reflection set quiet, centered, one breath.
4. **Color.** The Morning Verdict — field migrates bone→amber-dawn `#FBF2DF`, ink `#16120E`, Marigold `#E8930C` (glow) / `#B26F00` (text). Dawn-sweep gradient; no cold highlights.
5. **Scroll Experience.** `v` scrubs the settle to completion — the visitor watches consequence physically rearrange into calmer geometry; upward scroll replays it honestly.
6. **Transition.** In: Dissolve-to-Light completes from Decision. Out: **Migration** — dawn rises toward infinite warm light as the future's first line approaches.
7. **Interaction.** Hover an exhibit = "what changed" reveal; press = the lesson card behind it; the reflection beat is pure hold — read, no affordances competing.
8. **Why this is memorable.** Impact you can watch: the same wall from the archive sliding into calmer alignment as morning light crosses a ledger — proof delivered as geometry, not as a slide with an arrow.

## SCENE 7 — THE FUTURE

_Locked chain: five beats at f thresholds (.06/.28/.5/.7/.88) → dissolve steps → ember door → ember answer "The listening continues." → true fade → signature ≥3s. Anchors `fu:_` unchanged.\*

1. **Visual Concept — "Sunrise infinite."** Infinite warm light, infinite margins — one line at a time in ceremony center. The world dissolves behind: architecture (threads, exhibits) thins to near-nothing, then is gone. The ember door is a typographic threshold with a warm halo, door-shaped only by light. Signature set in the Analyst voice at exhibition size — plain, huge, calm.
2. **Motion Concept.** Beats crossfade at the locked thresholds — each line holds alone, world air around it. Warm-dissolve steps thin the field. The door warms on presence <100ms. Press: **The True Fade** — the only sanctioned pure `#000`, ≈2.5s — then the signature Mask-rises and holds ≥3s. Post-curtain furniture appears after a beat, calm text, one column.
3. **Typography.** The sparsest in the journey: Statement for the five beats at horizon scale; the closing couplet staged at reading distance, ≥2s hold before fade; signature in the Analyst voice — the plain title at monument scale is the entire positioning, typeset.
4. **Color.** Sunrise Infinite — `#FCFAF6`, ink `#14110E`, Sunrise Ember `#F26B3A` (glow) / `#D04E22` (text) — the Opening's pulse, grown into dawn. Sunrise radial whisper; no dark may return.
5. **Scroll Experience.** `f` advances the five beats; the runway releases at the ember answer; after the signature, a plain scroll column carries the real-door furniture — normalcy as the final generosity.
6. **Transition.** In: Migration completes into light. Out: The True Fade, then stillness. Nothing scrolls after meaning ends.
7. **Interaction.** Approach the door: it warms; press: the closing beat (Ω holds — it never opens, never teases, never becomes a form); the ember answer relays "The listening continues." All of it keyboard-identical, ≤3 interactive elements on stage at any moment.
8. **Why this is memorable.** A whole journey ends in five sentences, a door that warms but never opens, and a name the size of a monument in the quietest voice. The visitor closes the tab and immediately wants this person in the room.
