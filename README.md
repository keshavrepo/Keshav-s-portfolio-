# PROJECT K — The Portfolio of Keshav, Business Analyst

An interactive cinematic experience — not a resume, not a template — built on a written
creative foundation before any design or code exists.

## Documentation Index (Specification Series)

| Spec | Document | Status |
|---|---|---|
| SPEC-001 | [CREATIVE_VISION.md](./CREATIVE_VISION.md) — Creative Vision (concept, brand DNA, storytelling, IA, identity, positioning, creative law) | v1.0 — locked |
| SPEC-002 | [AUDIENCE_INTELLIGENCE.md](./AUDIENCE_INTELLIGENCE.md) — Audience Intelligence (ten personas, cross-analysis, twelve matrices, 300 operative rules) | v1.0 — binding foundation research |
| SPEC-003 | [STORY_BIBLE.md](./STORY_BIBLE.md) — Locked Story Bible refinement (chapter reviews, 100 storytelling rules, 100 never-dos, emotional & curiosity timelines, memory anchors, transition blueprint) | v1.0 — binding story-production law |

**Reading order:** SPEC-001 establishes what the experience must make people feel and the
principles of its expression (concept codename: **SIGNAL** — noise resolving into signal).
SPEC-002 establishes who the people are and the complete behavioral map of their visit.
SPEC-003 takes the approved story — the visitor's journey through a Business Analyst's mind,
from a heartbeat in the dark to the door that never opens — and hardens it for production
without touching its lock. Where documents meet: later specs deepen; they never overwrite.
