# PART I — THE TWENTY FOUNDATIONS

## 0. CHARTER — WHAT CHANGES. WHAT NEVER DOES.

**0.1 The reset in one line.**

Project K stops constructing worlds to be looked at, and starts speaking to be read — the mind is no longer _shown as architecture_; it _speaks as typography, moves as motion, feels as color, and travels as scroll_. Every chapter keeps its story; every beat keeps its seconds; every lock keeps its key. Only what the pixels are made of changes.

**0.2 What changes — the visual surface, exactly six things.**

1. **Rendering material.** Heavy WebGL-first world-building (neural fields as 3D space, station corridors, universe objects, engine housings) gives way to typography-led 2D composition: editorial spreads, kinetic type, drawn-line SVG, and capped canvas-2D atmosphere _where meaningful_.
2. **Color philosophy.** The single-signal monochrome-plus-ember system gives way to a premium, chapter-owned color constitution: seven palettes, seven emotional identities, one accent per chapter.
3. **Typography's rank.** From "primary material" (SPEC-001 §7.2) to _the architecture itself_: type now composes space, not just text. Massive display, variable axes, mask reveals, split lines — the sentence is the structure.
4. **Motion's rank.** From "second sacred material" to the hero: every interaction choreographed like a motion-design studio piece, inside the locked second-budgets.
5. **Texture register.** Paper-ledger-material heritage evolves into light-atmosphere texture: grain whispers, field fog, horizon gradients — all 2D, all cheap, all earned.
6. **Imagery policy.** Shifts but tightens: still no stock, no decoration; drawn-line evidence and type-posters inherit what 3D reliefs carried.

**0.3 What never changes — the immutable register.**

- The **story** and the chapter order: Opening → Mind → Business Thinking → Universe → Decision → Impact → Future (SPEC-003 six protected elements).
- Every **beat, beat budget, and second-contract** (S81/S88; the dive's 1.8–2.4s; suspense 1500–2000ms; corridor 3–6s; caption holds 4200ms; vista 1600ms; reveals 1400ms; react <100ms).
- The **interaction grammar**: hover reveals context · press expands reasoning · scroll progresses thinking — and no fourth verb (REUSABLES, interaction laws).
- The **journey physics**: one universe, one runway, one canvas host, one camera-idea; segments, locks (`GRAMMAR_LOCK_G`, `ENGINE_LOCK_W`), triggers (0.985 family), anchors (`op:* … fu:end`), the four tellings with full parity.
- The **accessibility covenant** entire (SPEC-005 §14, S94, the Semantic floor, reduced-motion telling, ≥12:1 body contrast, 44px, one aria-live voice).
- The **performance architecture** entire (budgets as code: ≤180KB initial route JS gzip, ≤1s first paint with authored pulse, Lighthouse floors, DPR clamp, demand frameloops, silence-by-default audio).
- The **voice** and every locked line of copy: the claim couplet (IR-45/S80), the stage beats, "The next decision is still waiting.", the signature "Keshav Choudhary — Business Analyst".
- The **identity**: plain Business Analyst; no invented grandeur; the door that never opens (Ω, N91).
- The **engineering**: tokens-driven Tailwind, conductor solo law, scene manifests, lifecycle, state stores, pipeline, gates (`lint` 0-warnings, `typecheck`, `build`), Conventional Commits.

**0.4 Resolutions of record (V2-T1 … V2-T7).** Prior visual law is never contradicted silently. Seven tensions between the locked documents and the V2 directive are resolved here, permanently:

- **V2-T1 — Typography's casting (SPEC-001 §7.2) → kept, upgraded.** The two-voice system (Statement / Analyst, caste discipline, numeral reverence) remains law. The V2 change is rank and scale: the Statement voice becomes structural (massive, masked, kinetic); the Analyst voice remains comprehension-invisible. Casting direction inside the locked temperament: optical-size variable serif for Statement (Fraunces-class), supreme-legibility sans for Analyst (Inter-class) — final faces are production decisions inside these walls.
- **V2-T2 — Color (SPEC-001 §7.3) → superseded as enumerated.** "Paper-and-ink pierced by one decisive color" is replaced by the chapter color constitution (§4). Two inheritances survive permanently: **warm-field physics** (warm near-blacks, warm off-whites, never sterile) and **the verdict-ration doctrine** (each chapter's accent is spent precisely the way the single signal color was: rarely, decisively). The red-pen ancestry is not lost — it is _chapter-scoped_ to Decision, where the verdict color finally owns its room.
- **V2-T3 — The RESOLUTION metaphor (SPEC-001 §8.1) → persists, re-dressed.** Noise→signal remains the structural metaphor at every zoom: it is the story itself. V2 changes its _rendering_: scatter-resolves-into-order is now performed by typography and motion (blur→sharp, scattered→aligned, dim→read) rather than by 3D fields. The sheriff's rules (§8.5) still hold.
- **V2-T4 — The four licensed metaphors (N61) → persist, 2D-native.** Mind-world stays a _place_ but is drawn in space and type, not meshes. Weather/fog becomes layout-density + grain + field-dimming (S38 weather-as-meter survives as the journey's only progress indicator). The red thread becomes the **drawn line** (SVG stroke, animated by draw-on). The door becomes a **typographic threshold with a warm halo**, still seam-less, still never opening. No fifth metaphor is introduced.
- **V2-T5 — Neurons and 3D hero-objects → retired as visual material, truth-binding preserved.** The story's neural world is re-rendered as a _thought-typography field_: every visible connection still terminates in a true line of inference (R-13/S11 survives verbatim) — the link is now a drawn thread and a labeled anchor, not a shader pathway. The T1 license (truth-binding) outlives the renderer.
- **V2-T6 — Engineering (SPEC-005) → fully preserved, payloads adapted.** JourneyRoot, runway, shared refs, projectors, conductor, Gated/ThoughtCard/scheduler, manifests, capability probe, stores, pipeline: unchanged. In V2 implementation phases, _payloads_ mounted into this machinery become 2D (DOM/CSS/SVG/canvas-2D); the plumbing is never redesigned to suit a look. SCENE-001…007 code, docs, and locks are never rewritten.
- **V2-T7 — Color is story-scheduled, never preference-scheduled.** Generalizing SPEC-001's dark-chapter law: the palette arc belongs to the narrative clock. No light/dark theme toggle exists, now or later. (Reduced-motion and motion preferences remain visitor-sovereign — those are accessibility, not theming.)

## 1. VISUAL PHILOSOPHY

**1.1 The conviction.** The 3D worlds tried to _show_ a mind from the inside. V2 discovers the stronger move, and the genre's highest craft tier already knows it: **a sentence arriving the way a thought arrives — masked, rising, settling — is worth more than any monument.** Typography is the architecture. Motion is the acting. Color is the emotion. The scroll is the camera. Composition is the set. The visitor should feel pages turning inside one seamless world, even though no page ever turns.

**1.2 From constructed worlds to authored spreads.** Every chapter is re-read as an editorial spread that happens to be alive: discipline of a magazine, physics of a stage, air of a gallery. Awwwards-tier craft is the _floor of execution_, never the subject; the subject remains a Business Analyst's thinking. V2 is the high end of design culture in service of clarity — not design culture performing itself.

**1.3 Reference study — three exemplars, philosophy extracted, pixels refused.**

**The "feels like" register** (Awwwards · Apple · Linear · Stripe · Framer · Nothing · Figma Config · Notion · modern editorial · high-end motion design), compressed to one line each: **Apple** — restraint as luxury, one act per screen. **Linear** — precision of type, spacing, and state; nothing wobbles. **Stripe** — editorial evidence design; numbers set like testimony. **Framer** — masks and clip-path choreography as native grammar. **Nothing** — hardware-honesty: a small dot of light can carry a whole identity. **Figma Config** — staged theatricality of keynote pacing. **Notion** — calm information confidence. **Modern editorial** — the spread is the unit of thought. **Awwwards/high-end motion** — surprise with purpose, craft as respect. Project K V2 borrows the discipline of all, the identity of none.

**1.4 The five V2 convictions.**

1. **The sentence is the building.** Type composes space; layout follows text, never text squeezed into layout.
2. **Motion is the difference between reading and watching.** Every transition is directed; the hand always feels the director knew where they were.
3. **Color is memory.** Seven palettes become seven rooms the visitor remembers (the loci doctrine re-cast in hue instead of geometry).
4. **Surprise lives in rhythm, not gimmicks.** The site surprises by changing tempo, scale, and light — never by breaking its voice.
5. **Craft respecting constraints _is_ the aesthetic.** The locked budgets, locks, and parity covenants are the tightrope that makes the walking worth watching.

**1.5 The V2 sins (named so review can kill them):** carousel-thinking (disposable spreads), demo-reel motion (effects hunting for meaning), gradient-soup (color without ration), Dribbble-fragmentation (every shot pretty, no story coherent), kinetic chaos (type performing acrobatics while meaning waits), neon "premium" (voltage instead of warmth), scroll-hijack cinema (the hand robbed of its dolly).

## 2. MOTION PHILOSOPHY

**2.1 Motion is the hero — and the hero still serves the story.** The locked law survives the promotion verbatim: _motion exists to make meaning felt; any motion that could be deleted without losing meaning is deleted_ (SPEC-001 §7.4). V2's elevation of motion is not a license for more motion; it is a demand for _authored_ motion — every frame directed, every easing chosen, every entrance cast.

**2.2 The physics stay locked.** Decelerating arrivals, instant reactions (S87); durations from tokens only (`react` 90ms · `ui` 200ms · `state` 420ms · `enter` 720ms · `pulse` 900ms · `dive` 2000ms inside 1800–2400 · `suspense` 1750ms inside 1500–2000 · reveals 1400ms · vista 1600ms · caption hold 4200ms · corridor 4500ms inside 3000–6000). Comedy physics remain banned (bounce, elastic, wobble). lagSmoothing doctrine: a dropped frame never becomes a time-jump.

**2.3 The Resolve is the house signature, now in 2D.** SPEC-001's kinetic signature — unrest to rest, quick to begin, long and quiet to settle — is performed by type and light: blurred words sharpen, scattered lines align, dim phrases read, noise exhales into signal. The visitor must recognize it by the second chapter as _the way this world settles_, and the journey's final resolution lands as the last of a recognizable family — rhyme, not repetition (§7.4 law carried into V2).

**2.4 One voice on stage.** The conductor persists: one solo per frame, ambient capped at three. Motion's hierarchy at any moment: **the solo > the hand (scrubbed acts) > ambient > stillness**. Stillness is a cast member: the Holds (suspense, cost-pause, caption holds, the destination-line) are motion's negative space, budgeted and protected like the silences they always were (S89).

**2.5 Reduced motion is the same film in stills.** Parity is not a downgrade path; it is a parallel telling authored with the same care (S94). Every kinetic act in this document is defined with its resolved still twin. Motion QA ships both.

## 3. TYPOGRAPHY PHILOSOPHY

**3.1 Typography is the architecture.** Not the primary "material" — the building. Spreads are composed from sentences outward; a screen of well-set type with everything else switched off must still feel like Project K (the §7.2 screenful test, sharpened into V2's first gate). Display type grows to architecture scale: hero words that define the composition's geography the way the old monuments did — but they say something, so they outlast something.

**3.2 The two voices, re-cast for scale.** The Statement voice (Fraunces-class optical serif) is the protagonist: assured, editorial, capable of carrying a locked claim line at 16vw without sweating. The Analyst voice (Inter-class) is the narrator: comprehension-invisible, numerals superb, warm at reading sizes. Caste discipline persists; the one V2 license new to the casting couch is mixed-voice kinetics — a grotesque line answered by an italic serif phrase inside one sentence, used as _contrast of temperament_, budgeted to hero moments (reference study §1.3).

**3.3 Kinetic type is acting, not effects.** Words appear the way thoughts do: masked from below the baseline, settling into stillness, never clowning. Splits happen at phrase boundaries; reading never waits for animation (N21); every act ends DOM-real and holds.

**3.4 Numerals remain sacred (SPEC-001 §7.2 verbatim).** Figures are testimony: tabular in evidence, display at verdict moments, land whole (count-ups are slot machines), provenance one gesture away. In V2 the honored numeral is the closest thing the design has to sculpture — the one permitted monument is a number that earned it (R-54).

**3.5 Craft beneath notice.** Hung punctuation, true dashes, curly quotes, optical margins, no widows at display, authored line-breaks reviewed like poetry. The fluent visitor feels expense without knowing why; the forensic visitor (P04) can go looking and find discipline.

## 4. COLOR PHILOSOPHY

**4.1 Color becomes part of storytelling — formally.** The monochrome-plus-signal era ends. Color is now the journey's emotional chronology: seven chapters own seven identities, so the visitor's memory of the journey has _rooms_ again (method-of-loci re-cast in hue). The arc moves **dark → dusk → daylight → deep archive → bone-and-red → dawn → infinite light**, mirroring the locked intensity curve (SPEC-003 §6) and the earned-calm ending.

**4.2 The constitution.** Six laws govern every palette: **(i)** one accent family per chapter; **(ii)** the accent is rationed (≈≤5% of pixels per view, one accent event per frame family — the one-ember law, generalized); **(iii)** warmth DNA across all hues — vibrant but premium; no cyberpunk neon, no rainbow chrome, no childish primaries; **(iv)** a chapter's full palette is field + ink + accent (glow-tier + text-tier) + at most one atmosphere gradient; **(v)** crossings are color _migrations_ — time-based crossfades of entire fields — never hard cuts, except the single sanctioned Fade to True Black at the end; **(vi)** color is story-scheduled, never preference-scheduled (V2-T7).

**4.3 The seven chapter constitutions.** (Candidate values are production-grade direction; final ramps are token work with measured ratios — V-C register binds usage.)

**I. OPENING — "The First Light."** Emotion: alerted calm in the dark.
Field: warm void `#0E0C0A` · Ink: warm paper `#F4EDE4` · Accent: **Pulse Ember** glow `#FF7A3D`, text-tier `#FF9A66` · Gradient: none (void purity). Story: one warm signal in the dark; everything else is silence. Law: no second hue may enter the void.

**II. MIND — "The Dusk Inside."** Emotion: hospitable curiosity, interiority.
Field: midnight iris `#171432` · Ink: `#ECE9F6` · Accent: **Idea Violet** glow `#8B7CF6`, text-tier `#A99CFF` · Gradient: whisper iris→plum `#241E4A`. Story: dusk inside a thinking room — thoughts visible because the room is dim. Law: no daylight white fields here.

**III. GRAMMAR — "Daylight Thinking."** Emotion: dawning comprehension.
Field: warm paper `#F5F0E8` · Ink: `#171310` · Accent: **Evergreen** glow `#12856A`, text-tier `#0C6B53` · Gradient: paper→pale moss `#EDF2EA`. Story: understanding grows in daylight; the four verbs stand in clear air. Law: no red here — grammar never threatens.

**IV. UNIVERSE — "The Brass Archive."** Emotion: proof, altitude, the record.
Field: deep marine `#0E1B2A` · Ink: `#EDF2F7` · Accent: **Archive Brass** glow `#E0B24A`, text-tier `#E6BC5E` · Gradient: marine→ink-slate. Story: the record room under brass lamps; honored numerals read like engraved plates. Law: no warm-paper fields — the record is cool order after daylight.

**V. DECISION — "The Red Pen."** Emotion: held breath; responsibility.
Field: bone `#F7F4EF` · Ink: `#141110` · Accent: **Verdict Crimson** glow `#D13B3B`, text-tier `#B02A2E` · Gradient: none — a cliff has no weather; red ration ≤3%. Story: one red pen in a quiet room; SPEC-001's annotation-pen ancestry returns at chapter scale. Law: gradients banned entirely in this chapter.

**VI. IMPACT — "The Morning Verdict."** Emotion: warm resolution; proof as transformation.
Field: migrates bone→amber-dawn `#FBF2DF` · Ink: `#16120E` · Accent: **Marigold** glow `#E8930C`, text-tier `#B26F00` · Gradient: dawn sweep amber→paper. Story: light becomes architecture — in 2D, light becomes _layout warmed from within_; the aftermath has morning in it. Law: no cold highlights.

**VII. FUTURE — "Sunrise Infinite."** Emotion: poised hope; typography as horizon.
Field: infinite warm light `#FCFAF6` · Ink: `#14110E` · Accent: **Sunrise Ember** `#F26B3A`, text-tier `#D04E22` · Gradient: sunrise radial (ember→gold→light) as atmosphere only. Story: the Opening's pulse ember, grown into dawn — **the bookend completed in color** (V-C73 keeps one ember hue constant through the whole journey for exactly this payoff). Law: no dark field may return here.

**4.4 Continuity with the old law (V2-T2).** The verdict-ration doctrine is inherited intact — each chapter simply owns its own pen. Warm-field physics (never pure black except the final fade; never sterile white; warm text tints on dark) is inherited intact. Contrast doctrine (§4 of ARCHITECTURE: body ≥12:1, accent-on-neutral reserved for display and affordance) is inherited and re-proven per palette (V-C register).

**4.5 What color refuses.** Color-coded KPI chrome, RAG status systems, category hues for data, "gradient mesh" wallpaper, duotone stock, neon on dark as a substitute for atmosphere, and any palette added for a season, campaign, or award deadline (N94 analog).

## 5. LAYOUT PHILOSOPHY

**5.1 The spread, not the canvas.** The unit of V2 composition is the editorial spread: asymmetric, grid-disciplined, typography-led, one dominant act per screen. The old worlds placed objects in space; V2 places _attention_ on a page that breathes. Layouts become storytelling: each chapter owns a signature composition (§V-L register L-V), recognized at a glance, rotated chapter-to-chapter so the journey's _shape_ keeps surprising while its _system_ stays one hand.

**5.2 Air is the second material.** Negative space is load-bearing: hero spreads carry ≥40% air; density and air alternate like movements (SPEC-001 §3.8 pacing physics translated to layout). Chapter air tokens and section-y rhythm persist from the spacing system; V2 spends them more theatrically.

**5.3 No boxes where hairlines will do.** The house separates with rules, rails, and white space before containers; card grids remain banned (story law generalised: projects are not cards → meaning is not boxed). Evidence renders as exhibits: label / figure / source on one axis.

**5.4 Furniture stays furniture.** Quiet identity mark margin-top-left (T5); captions on the lower-third rail; one aria-live voice with a fixed visual echo; express/settings furniture lower-half on phones (ER-82). Nothing sticky follows the reading; the journey is the chrome.

**5.5 The fold is abolished as a layout law.** Compositions flow; nothing meaningful depends on being above a viewport crease. The runway's segments map to full-height spreads (_one spread per beat_), so scroll position and composition stay phase-locked by design.

## 6. SCROLL PHILOSOPHY

**6.1 The hand is the camera.** Scroll is the dolly track of V2: continuous, reversible, owned by the visitor's hand. The runway is engineering law — segments, lengths, locks (`GRAMMAR_LOCK_G 0.94` needs `gm:decision:committed`; `ENGINE_LOCK_W 0.92` needs `de:committed:*`), triggers (0.985 family), and the channels (`p,g,u,w,v,f`) are served by the skin, never re-timed by it.

**6.2 Scrubbed, not triggered.** Continuous acts bind to hand position (hand-position = frame-position); single-fire entrances complete and rest. Scrub smoothing stays ≤120ms so the hand never feels translated. Scrolling up re-reads a consistent world (causal replay, NR-61).

**6.3 Chapters are composition changes, not page turns.** One universe continues evolving (locked): crossings happen as masks opening, threads drawing across, and color fields migrating — never as cuts. The journey reads as one long, directed exhale with alternating dense/air spreads.

**6.4 Progress is felt, never instrumented.** No progress bars, percentages, or meters (S38): fog visibly thins, fields visibly warm, the thread visibly lengthens — the world is the meter, as it always was. Fast flicks never skip locked beats; the machine's clamps, not the skin, decide what can be outrun.

**6.5 Rest is designed into the track.** Vista spreads (1600ms of whole-composition), corridors (3–6s of air), caption holds (4200ms alone), and text acts that complete by 80% of their range (20% headroom for the eye to land). Pacing is a promise; the scroll keeps it.

## 8. INTERACTION LANGUAGE

**8.1 The grammar is locked — and it is enough.** Hover reveals hidden context (the world admits what it knows). Press expands reasoning (the ThoughtCard carries one idea, calm, closable) or commits (the decision: irreversible, staged, suspense-held). Scroll progresses thinking. V2 makes this triad feel _expensive_: <100ms acknowledgment, effortless curves, states named and styled (hover / focus-visible / active / locked / earned).

**8.2 Doors are typographic actors.** Approach-warmth (hover halo, glow-tier ration), invite (the earned label), commit (press) — ≥44px targets, keyboard-identical (S5), never mystery meat. The ember door at the end warms on approach and, on press, plays the closing beat. It never opens into a form: Ω holds (N91/N92); the real door exists post-signature as calm furniture.

**8.3 The world notices the visitor.** Presence reactivity (the cursor's attention feeds atmosphere, <100ms) survives as the "reading light": a soft chapter-tinted halo that lifts fog where attention lands — attention made visible, never a trail, never a chase. Coarse pointers receive the tap-mapped twin; reduced motion receives the still disc.

**8.4 The machine speaks in the world's voice.** Hints escalate ambient→suggestive→direct in margin lines, never modals (S37); errors are courteous, specific, blameless (N79); the single aria-live voice announces beats without chatter; idle is data, not failure (S85).

**8.5 Restraint is the premium.** No modals, no gamified feedback, no dark patterns, no dossier personalization, no take-backs on earned moments. Every touch answered calmly, instantly, honestly — that's the entire trick, done perfectly.

## 9. TRANSITION LANGUAGE

Crossings are the story's bloodstream (T0–T11, SPEC-003 §9); V2 gives them a six-move grammar. Durations remain contracts; only materials change.

| Move                      | Mechanic                                                                                        | Serves                                                                      |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The Iris**              | A clip-path aperture opens around the pulse point; the world arrives through it                 | T2 the dive (1.8–2.4s, decelerating, locked envelope)                       |
| **The Wipe**              | Masked spread-to-spread passage along the reading gravity                                       | T4–T6 chapter advances inside a segment                                     |

(iii) Locks express as settle-back + one calm line, never a janky clamp. (iv) Mystery budget stands — ≤2s unexplained motion anywhere (S82). (v) Each crossing ships its reduced-telling twin (opacity-only or instant). (vi) A transition that overruns its budget is triaged as a story defect, not polish debt (S88).

## 10. GRID SYSTEM

**10.1 The skeleton.** A 12-column fluid grid; margins tokenized `clamp(20px → 56px)`; gutters `clamp(16px → 32px)`; content field max ≈ 1520–1600px with licensed full-bleed breakouts for display and atmosphere; baseline rhythm on the untouched 4px scale (ARCHITECTURE §5).

**10.2 The columns' castes.** Body measure occupies 6 of 12 columns (60–68ch); evidence exhibits occupy 8; display spans up to all 12 with air on both flanks. Collapse law: 12 → 8 (tablet) → 4 (phone), composition _intent_ surviving collapse — hierarchy transposes, never re-authors.

**10.3 Rails.** Named vertical axes carry the furniture: the margin rail (eyebrows, identity mark, marginalia), the lower-third rail (captions, status echo), the action rail (doors, commits). A spread passes review only when its alignment axes can be named out loud.

**10.4 Breakpoints.** `sm 640 / md 768 / lg 1024 / xl 1440` — tokenized, no customs. Fluid `clamp()` typography and gutters interpolate continuously between them; the music transposes, it never re-scores.

**10.5 Tension is designed.** Centered ceremony is rationed to the Opening void and the signature (bookend geometry); everything else aligns to edges and rails. One rule-broken element per chapter, earned by its beat, is the licensed surprise.

## 11. SPACING SYSTEM

**11.1 The base persists.** Tailwind's 4px scale is the atom (ARCHITECTURE §5 unchanged); layout tokens (`gutter`, `section-y`, `measure-gap`) remain the only lawful layout distances; arbitrary values for layout are build-level sins, optical nudges carry spec citations (§2 law reused).

**11.2 V2 cadence.** Section-y is spent theatrically: hero spreads take the upper band (`clamp(96px → 200px)`), air spreads beyond it, dense evidence spreads below it — the _alternation_ is the rhythm (dense/air duet, §5.2). Chapter air between spreads of one segment is a composition decision; chapter crossings add the Migration's field breath on top.

**11.3 Type-adjacent spacing is typographic.** Space around display lines is measured in the type's own leading rhythms (space follows the em, not the viewport); measure-gap inside a text column governs all paragraph stacks; captions sit a fixed multiplier from the lower-third rail.

**11.4 Touch geometry is spacing.** The ≥44px target and 8px separation laws are _spacing rules with legal force_; invisible padding counts toward them and is part of spacing design, not an accessibility patch.

**11.5 Spacing QA.** Rag, air-ratio (hero ≥40%), and separation audits are measured, not eyeballed; screenshot baselines per beat guard drift scene over scene.

## 12. VISUAL HIERARCHY

**12.1 The one-act law.** Every spread has one thing it exists to make felt — cast as the hero (a claim, a numeral, a door, a line). Everything else ranks beneath it in a legible order: **hero → orientation (eyebrow/rail) → support (body/exhibit) → furniture**. Two heroes is a fight; three is a riot.

**12.2 Hierarchy is built with size, weight, and space — color last** (SPEC-001 §7.2 inherited). The accent hue marks the actionable and the verdict, inside its ration; tone (ink-alpha steps) builds the quiet ranks. If hierarchy only works in color, it doesn't work (grayscale survival test, V-C54).

**12.3 The scale duet.** V2's signature hierarchy device is extreme scale contrast: a display element 6–10× its subordinate. The duet creates instant reading order the visitor feels rather than parses — hierarchy as physical law.

**12.4 Reading gravity.** LTR gravity starts at the title rail, lands on the hero, falls to support, and ends where the next scroll beckons — the composition always aims down-journey. Captions and status echo hold fixed positions across chapters so the peripheral grammar never re-teaches itself.

**12.5 Hierarchy across the four tellings.** Cinematic, stills, express, and semantic tellings share one hierarchy described three ways (visual, order, semantics): same hero, same order, same weight — the parity covenant applies to rank, not just content.

**13.5 Data graphics, where evidence takes visual form** (archive/exhibit layer), remain _evidence-beautiful_: minimal chrome, every mark earning ink, labeled like exhibits (§7.7 inherited verbatim).

## 14. ILLUSTRATION PHILOSOPHY

**14.1 The drawn line is the only illustrator.** SVG stroke-work in one consistent hand: the thread, underlines, marginalia marks, exhibit diagrams, the rail. Drawn-on (The Draw) or still; never clip-art, never blob-gradients, never a second illustration style.

**14.2 Marginalia persists as the graphic voice of thinking** (SPEC-001 §8.2 unchanged in content, re-rendered in 2D): annotations, underlines, struck-through claims replaced by better ones — always _content_, always true about what it touches, rationed like the red pen it descends from. Now drawn as strokes (no handwriting fonts: type never impersonates a hand).

**14.3 Diagrams are exhibits, not explanations.** A figure shows what it must show, labeled like a courtroom exhibit, drawn in the chapter's ink with accent used at verdict ration. Nothing schematic-cosmic, nothing decorative-geometric.

**14.4 Icons are not illustration** (§15 governs them); atmosphere is not illustration (§17 governs it); display type used as shape is not illustration — it is typography doing architecture's job, and §3 governs it.

**14.5 Illustration QA.** One-hand test (all strokes could be one author), truth test (every mark says something true), ration audit (a spread drowning in marks fails), reduced-motion twin for every drawn-on sequence.

## 15. ICONOGRAPHY PHILOSOPHY

**15.1 Icons are functional grammar, not decoration.** The system admits a closed set — door, arrow (direction of journey), marker (location/location-furniture), close, plus/minus for disclosure, the pulse dot — drawn from one stroke: 1.5px at 20px grid, round caps, square joins avoided, geometry aligned to the baseline grid.

**15.2 Behavior.** Icons inherit ink by default, chapter accent only when carrying the one offered action; hover/focus states shift along the chapter ramp; Draw-on only where the icon _is_ a door beat; never animated decoratively, never emoji-adjacent, never an icon where a word is clearer (words win ties — a Business Analyst labels axes).

**15.3 Placement law.** Icons serve the rails and the furniture; body text never contains icons mid-sentence; target sizing follows the 44px law with invisible padding.

**15.4 Bans.** Logo walls as proof (witnesses are staged at turns by story law, not tiled), tech-stack badge rows (N10/B-adjacency), social-icon clusters (V2 directive + post-curtain calm: the real door is a named contact route, not a profile parade), hamburgers hiding furniture, and any icon invented for symmetry with a neighbor.

**15.5 Icon QA.** Stroke consistency audit, meaning test (labeled or unambiguous), contrast proof (icons ≥3:1), parity (icons are aria-hidden décor with labeled controls beneath).

## 16. CURSOR PHILOSOPHY

**16.1 The cursor is a reading light.** Presence reactivity (R-04's <100ms world-answers-you, the memory anchor of the Opening) survives V2 as a soft, chapter-tinted halo: attention made visible. Where the reader looks, fog lifts a little; where the hand goes, door-beats warm. The cursor never entertains; it _accompanies_.

**16.2 Four named states, four behaviors.** `read` (platform cursor + faint field halo), `door` (halo concentrates, edge of warmth), `press` (contraction ≤90ms), `hold` (locked-segment stillness + the settle-back). Morphs acknowledge within the react band; nothing overshoots.

**16.3 Physics.** Halo radius ≤240px, alpha ≤0.35; magnetic pull ≤6px with instant settle, never on text; no trails, no drag lag, no hiding of the native cursor without its exact replacement, hit-testing on the element, not the graphic.

**16.4 Parity across bodies.** Coarse pointers: no cursor layer — presence arrives through touch physics; reduced motion: static disc or off per setting; the World never _requires_ the cursor (all reveals reachable by focus and by tap-mapped equivalents); presence data never leaves the machine (S95's technical twin).

**16.5 Cursor QA.** <100ms acknowledgment trace, contrast on every field (dark and light), target-obscuration audit, solo-law (halo dims during solos and holds), battery (no per-frame cost beyond the shared rAF tick).

## 17. NOISE & TEXTURE PHILOSOPHY

**17.1 Texture is light, 2D, and cheap.** The paper-ledger heritage evolves into _atmosphere_: a whisper of grain (static SVG filter layer, opacity ≤0.06), chapter-tinted field gradients (the weather), and the horizon luminance of calm endings. Nothing three-dimensional, nothing photographic, nothing that costs a compositing budget it can't defend.

**17.2 Fog is texture with meaning.** The weather/fog metaphor renders as _density_: heavier grain, compressed values, dimmed fields in turbulence; thinning grain and rising lightness as understanding resolves (S38 — the world's condition is the only progress indicator). Fog is never blur-soup and never weather-channel clip-art (N64).

**17.3 Grain law.** Static, not animated (animated noise is vestibular strobing, banned); procedurally generated, zero binary bloat (the existing pipeline pattern persists); never over text-bearing regions below contrast law; uniform in character across chapters, tinted to the field.

**17.4 Atmosphere law.** Gradients drift within 4% of their field's saturation/value; light-leaks serve calm and dawn (Impact, Future) and are banned elsewhere; every atmosphere declares its canvas budget and degrades first under load (the governor's first ration).

**17.5 Texture QA.** Still-frame beauty (each beat a poster), contrast continuity through gradients, performance trace (texture never the long-pole), reduced twin (texture minus motion is _texture_, so parity is natural — motionless texture stays).

## 19. PERFORMANCE PRESERVATION

**19.1 The budgets are law, quoted:** first paint ≤1000ms with the authored pulse · input latency <100ms · initial route JS ≤180KB gzip · route JS ceiling 350KB gzip · Lighthouse floors (A11y 100, SEO 100, BP ≥95, Perf ≥90) · budgets as code, asserted in production config · zero trackers. **V2 changes nothing above; V2's visual material changes _around_ the budgets, and must leave headroom, not consume it.**

**19.2 The V2 weight thesis — lighter, not heavier.** The 3D-first payloads retire in favor of DOM/CSS/SVG/canvas-2D payloads; display typography is the hero and it is _text_. The expected direction of every V2 visual phase is First-Load-JS _down_ or flat versus the 169KB V1 watermark; any phase that regresses budget must say so, prove why, and get rejected unless the reason is narrative law.

**19.3 The cheap-motion discipline.** On-screen motion animates `transform` and `opacity` (plus font-variation on display elements); filters (blur etc.) never scrub with scroll; `will-change` is surgical; shared rAF refs persist (zero React renders per pointer/scroll frame — architecture law; the skin never regresses it); `frameloop="demand"`-equivalent discipline for all canvas; DPR clamp; ambient killed offscreen; lagSmoothing; battery test (10-minute idle ≈ silence).

**19.4 Type performance.** Fonts remain self-hosted, subset, preloaded on routes that use them; metric-compatible fallbacks make swap invisible on locked lines; display scale is computed, not resource-extra — one variable file per voice, full stop.

**19.5 Verification unchanged.** `lint` (0 warnings) · `typecheck` · `build` green · SSR semantic smoke per scene · Web Vitals report wired but silent by default. Every quality gate from V1 applies to V2 phases; the skin sits in the same gym as the old scenes.

## 20. FINAL DESIGN RULES

Twenty commandments that outrank taste, including the author's:

1. **The story is final.** A visual idea that needs a story change is dead on arrival.
2. **The seconds are final.** No look may stretch, squeeze, or skip a locked beat.
3. **The hand is sovereign.** Motion, scroll, and interaction serve the visitor's intent — never argue with it.
4. **The sentence is the building.** If a spread needs 3D to impress, the sentence wasn't strong enough; fix the sentence.
5. **One act per screen.** Two heroes is a fight; the visitor came for one beat at a time.
6. **Color is memory.** Seven chapters, seven owned palettes; a palette borrowed from a neighbor is a plot hole.
7. **The accent is a verdict.** Rationed, decisive, measured — spent like the story's rarest currency.
8. **Motion must survive the deletable test.** Meaning felt, or it never renders.
9. **Every state is beautiful at rest.** Stillness is the requirement; animation is the privilege.
10. **The Resolve is the autograph.** Unrest to rest, quick to begin, long to settle — recognizable by chapter two.
11. **Surprise lives in rhythm.** Tempo, scale, and light change; the voice never does.
12. **The grid is felt, not seen.** Discipline underneath; life on top.
13. **Nothing decorates.** Every mark says something true or it is taxed out of existence.
14. **Numerals are sacred.** Testimony typesetting, verdict staging, provenance one gesture away.
15. **The door never opens.** Not for a season, not for a metric, not for a jury.
16. **Parity ships together.** Cinematic, stills, express, semantic — or the scene doesn't ship.
17. **The budgets are the brief.** 180KB, 1 second, 100ms — V2 is the discipline that makes craft _possible_.
18. **Four gaits or it didn't happen.** Fast flick, slow read, keyboard-only, touch-only — plus AT, before merge.
19. **A fragment must accuse us.** Any cropped screenshot, found unattributed, should still read as Keshav's (the §7.9 fragment test, V2's north star).
20. **Behave like the subject.** Clear, calm, honest, fast where it matters, complete where it counts — the medium remains the interview (S100).

## REGISTER V-M — 100 MOTION RULES

_Motion is the hero; the hero serves the story. Ten chapters of ten._

### V-M-I. The constitution (1–10)

**V-M1.** Motion is meaning performed or it is nothing. Because the deletable test is the hero's vow of poverty: any motion whose removal loses no meaning is removed (SPEC-001 §7.4).

**V-M2.** The stillness clause governs every frame: every state of every moment must be beautiful completely at rest. Because stillness is the accessibility covenant, the performance covenant, and the guarantee against time (§7.4).

**V-M3.** Exactly one primary motion performs at any moment; ambient caps at three concurrent. Because the conductor is architecture, not advice (SPEC-005 §7; PC-10/EG-7).

**V-M4.** Durations come from motion tokens, never literals. Because the locked second-budgets are story law (S81/S88): react 90 · ui 200 · state 420 · enter 720 · pulse 900 · dive 2000 (1800–2400) · suspense 1750 (1500–2000) · reveals 1400 · vista 1600 · caption hold 4200 · corridor 4500 (3000–6000).

**V-M5.** Decelerating arrivals, instant reactions. Because the world takes its time _for_ the visitor and never _from_ the visitor (S87).

**V-M6.** Motion never gates meaning: content exists before, during, and after its animation. Because fact-gating-by-theater kills the fastest audiences (N21/N88); the SSR floor carries every beat at rest.

**V-M7.** The visitor's hand outranks every timeline. Because touch-betrayal is the one unforgivable kinetic crime (N26/§7.4).

**V-M8.** A beat owns a second-budget before it owns a keyframe. Because unbudgeted beauty is where bloat hides (S81).

**V-M9.** Reduced motion is a parallel telling, authored, never a downgrade. Because parity-after-launch isn't parity (S94).

**V-M10.** Nothing performs unannounced: every motion has a narrative caller. Because surprise-behavior teaches caution, and cautious visitors stop touching (S92).

### V-M-II. Easing and duration discipline (11–20)

**V-M11.** Entrances use the house curve (`signal-out`: fast in, long settle) and nothing else. Because the Resolve must be recognizable as footsteps (§7.4).

**V-M12.** Settles and exits use the resolve-in curve; crossfades use standard. Because three curves are a language; twelve is an accent nobody learns.

**V-M13.** Custom cubic-bezier values are banned outside tokens. Because easing-by-vibe breaks the family (SPEC-005 §10 law).

**V-M14.** Duration scales with travel distance, bounded by token bands. Because a dot crossing 20px and a field crossing the viewport are different sentences wearing the same grammar.

**V-M15.** React ≤100ms · hover band 90–200ms · state 420ms · entrance 720ms. Because each band encodes a promise: acknowledgment, preview, change, arrival (ER-3 and scene law).

**V-M16.** Anything beyond 2.5s must cite a locked beat. Because secondary durations are how mystery overruns its welcome (S82).

**V-M17.** Ease-in entrances are banned. Because arriving reluctantly reads as the interface not wanting to be there.

**V-M18.** Stagger steps live 30–60ms. Because below 30ms is a blur and above 60ms is a queue.

**V-M19.** A dropped frame never becomes a time-jump. Because the story must never lurch (lagSmoothing doctrine, SPEC-005 §7).

**V-M20.** Duration review asks one question: chosen or tolerated? Because the visitor can always tell the difference.

### V-M-III. The house vocabulary (21–30)

**V-M21.** The Pulse is the only licensed infinite loop: 900ms, ember, heartbeat. Because the leitmotif lives or dies by scarcity (§7 law 1; the pulse that beats everywhere beats nowhere).

**V-M22.** The Mask reveals text: overflow-hidden rises and clip-path wipes, anchored at the baseline. Because thoughts surface from below; nothing rains from the sky in a calm world.

**V-M23.** The Split divides at phrase boundaries only — words and lines; letters rarely. Because a split mid-phrase is a split infinitive: a voice crime (§V-T32).

**V-M24.** The Draw animates strokes by dash-offset: thread, underlines, arrows, diagram lines. Because the red thread deserves a hand, not a renderer (V2-T4).

**V-M25.** The Resolve gathers scatter into order: blur sharpens, fragments align, dim reads. Because noise→signal is the whole brand (§8.1/V2-T3).

**V-M26.** The Migration crossfades whole color-fields across chapter boundaries. Because crossings are weather changes, not page turns (§6.1/T-family).

**V-M27.** The Settle re-composes after commitment: layout glides to calmer targets, affected first, staggered. Because impact is transformation rendered as architecture-in-type (SCENE-006 law re-dressed).

**V-M28.** The Murmur is capped canvas-2D ambient: fog grain, pulse dust, horizon light — nothing else. Because ambient complexity was the old genre's schizophrenia; ours has a name and a budget.

**V-M29.** The Breath oscillates hero compositions ≤2% over 3–6s. Because held moments should feel inhabited, not paused.

**V-M30.** The Cascade staggers serial entrances, one instrument at a time. Because orchestration is one voice plus a holding orchestra (§7.4 discipline).

### V-M-IV. Typography in motion (31–40) — see V-T-IV for setting law

**V-M31.** Lines are the unit of choreography; below-the-word animation is lawful but rationed. Because the visitor reads lines, not particles.

**V-M32.** Lines rise from below their own baseline (24–40% of line height). Because surfacing is thought-physics; dropping is gravity-physics, and gravity is slapstick here.

**V-M33.** Rotation on rising lines caps at 6° and returns to zero. Because spin is comedy; tilt is inflection.

**V-M34.** Kinetic type is legible at every t: no unreadable intermediate lasts past the react band. Because smeared words are broken words (N21-family).

**V-M35.** Font-axis animation (wght/wdth/opsz) is display-only, solo-ranked, ≤720ms. Because the letterform breathing is theater; theater takes the stage alone.

**V-M36.** Animated letter-spacing is banned. Because tracking shimmer reads as a rendering bug wearing cologne.

**V-M37.** A line that lands holds one breath before anything beneath it moves. Because arrival deserves acknowledgment (caption-hold law family).

**V-M38.** Variable-weight breathing on display is ±30 units maximum. Because beyond that the type strobe-lights the room.

**V-M39.** Every kinetic act ends DOM-real: composed, selectable, findable. Because the browser must never re-flow at the end of a trick (and AT must own the same words).

**V-M40.** Every kinetic act ships an instant still twin. Because the reduced telling is cast from the same script (S94/V-T40).

### V-M-V. Scroll-coupled motion (41–50)

**V-M41.** Scroll-driven means scrubbed: hand-position = frame-position. Because the scroll is the dolly, and a dolly never teleports (§6.2).

**V-M42.** Scrub smoothing stays ≤120ms. Because heavier smoothing is the system arguing with the hand (N26 twin).

**V-M43.** Single-fire entrances triggered on scroll complete ≤720ms after trigger and rest. Because triggered loops under the wheel are slot machines.

**V-M44.** Every scrubbed act is fully reversible by scrolling up. Because traversal replays causally (NR-61); the world remembers honestly in both directions.

**V-M45.** Text acts complete by 80% of their scroll range — 20% rest headroom, always. Because the eye needs a runway to land on (§6.5).

**V-M46.** Parallax relatives stay within 1.12× ratio, display elements only; body text never parallaxes. Because body copy that drifts makes readers seasick and graders suspicious.

**V-M47.** The skin adds zero pins: pinning is the runway's engineering. Because holds are story physics, authored and locked (§0.3).

**V-M48.** During any pinned hold, exactly one element scrubs. Because the solo law applies at scroll scale too.

**V-M49.** Velocity may intensify ambient; it may never accelerate content past a locked beat. Because the machine's clamps, not the skin, decide what can be outrun (V-S20).

**V-M50.** When the hand stops, motion eases to rest within 420ms. Because idling is reading time, and reading time is sacred (S85-adjacent).

### V-M-VI. Ambient and atmosphere (51–60)

**V-M51.** Ambient is weather, never plot: opacity ≤0.35, amplitude ≤8px. Because ambient that demands attention is a solo in disguise, and it will be tried as one.

**V-M52.** Ambient concurrency caps at three. Because the conductor budgets air the way it budgets solos.

**V-M53.** Ambient dims or pauses while a solo performs. Because the orchestra holds (§7.4).

**V-M54.** Off-screen ambient is killed. Because battery is a design value and the canonical test room is an aisle seat (P08.0/ER-85).

**V-M55.** Canvas particles are meaningful-only: fog grain, pulse dust, horizon light. Because decoration particles were the first thing the V2 directive retired.

**V-M56.** Ambient loops run aperiodically or past 19s before repeating. Because a visible loop-seam teaches the visitor the world is a screensaver (S3.1 analog).

**V-M57.** Ambient yields to presence within 100ms. Because "it noticed me" dies in queue (R-04/S4).

**V-M58.** Grain is static; animated noise is banned. Because flicker-fields are vestibular events, not texture (§17.3).

**V-M59.** Atmosphere gradients drift ≤4% saturation/value. Because color weather should be felt on the skin, not watched on a screen.

**V-M60.** The default ambient state is stillness. Because motion must argue its way into the room, not squat in it.

### V-M-VII. Staging and narrative beats (61–70)

**V-M61.** The three silence-claps stay rationed (reframe, anti-sell turn, verdict). Because a fourth staged silence cheapens all three (S89).

**V-M62.** The suspense hold (1750ms, dev-asserted 1500–2000) is visually total: nothing animates during it. Because stillness is the effect (R-59/S55).

**V-M63.** Caption holds (4200ms) sit alone: nothing new enters beneath a holding line. Because a held line is the stage; the next actor waits in the wing.

**V-M64.** Vista beats (1600ms) show the whole spread before any detail moves. Because bearings before beauty (R-15 law, spread re-cast).

**V-M65.** Chapter reveals run inside their manifest budget (1400ms). Because the entrance budget is engineering law, not a suggestion (SPEC-005 §8).

**V-M66.** A locked segment resists as settle-back plus one calm line — never as a janky clamp. Because the world explains; the UI never scolds (S37/N28).

**V-M67.** Earned reveals use the Resolve: alpha arriving like signal from noise. Because everything appears after it is earned, and appears _earned_ (S28; glow-point law re-dressed).

**V-M68.** Under every pause-law state, the heartbeat persists at 25% intensity. Because the world holding its breath still has a pulse (world pause law, re-dressed).

**V-M69.** A solo dies cleanly: handoff window or death, never a ghost tween. Because nothing outlives its scene (scheduler/lifecycle law).

**V-M70.** Beat reviews score H/C/E/A/X per frame. Because motion is narrative QA before it is polish (SPEC-003 rubric adopted).

### V-M-VIII. Reduced motion and stills (71–80)

**V-M71.** Every beat in the full telling ships a resolved still in the reduced telling. Because four tellings ship together or not at all (S94).

### V-C-XI. Evidence and meaning (66–75)

### V-C-XII. Dark and light physics (76–87)

**V-C76.** Dark fields are warm near-blacks; pure `#000` exists exactly once — the final fade. Because true black is the story's last frame and cheapening it earlier is theft from the ending (S69).

**V-C78.** Text on dark fields is warm-tinted paper, never pure white. Because pure white on black is glare, and glare is violence at reading distance.

**V-C79.** Elevation on dark = lightness steps; on light = hairlines and space; drop-shadow budget ≤3 per view. Because gradient-depth is mood-lighting's debt collector.

**V-C81.** OLED-true black goes unused except the final fade. Because physics serve the story, not the panel (V-C76's corollary).

**V-C82.** Highlights simulate light, not glare: per-chapter luminance ceilings hold. Because sparkle is how interfaces sunburn.

**V-C83.** The one license to be vivid is the accent at glow-tier, inside its ration. Because vividness through scarcity is the entire trick (V-C4).

**V-C84.** Atmosphere gradients drift within 4% of the field's value/saturation. Because weather you can watch is weather that wanders off-meaning (V-M59's color statement).

**V-C85.** Grain and atmosphere never drop text regions below contrast floors. Because texture is cheap theater until it dims the words; then it's sabotage (§17.3).

**V-C86.** Photography, if it ever exists, grades toward the chapter palette. Because an ungraded photo in a color story is a footnote from another book (§13.2).

**V-C87.** OG/share artifacts use the resting palette. Because identity that shifts per channel is identity that isn't (§13.3/V-C65).

### V-C-XIII. Governance and QA (88–100)

**V-C94.** Migration midpoints are frame-sampled for contrast (start/mid/end minimum). Because the middle of the crossfade is where colors lie (V-C51).

**V-C95.** The palette arc is reviewed as a storyboard: seven fields in order must tell the emotion without a word. Because color is the chronology (V-C1/C2).

**V-C97.** Accent rations are measured per view at review. Because the eye lies about percentage and numbers don't (C17-audit discipline).

**V-C98.** No palette additions for seasons, campaigns, or award deadlines. Because the colors are archive-law, like the door (N94 analog).

**V-C99.** Annual review asks of every palette: still true, still ours? Because metaphors — and colors — are employees with performance reviews, not monuments (§8.5 rule 5).

**V-C100.** Above all, color behaves like its subject: vibrant where it counts, calm where it counts, honest everywhere. Because S100 has a chromatic voice too.

**V-S43.** Scrub transforms are composite-only: transform and opacity. Because paint-by-layout is jank on rails (V-M91).

**V-S44.** No layout reads inside the scroll handler; geometry caches at resize. Because getBoundingClientRect per frame is a tax with no receipt.

**V-S45.** Scrub-linked states settle with token easings; linear mapping is reserved for literal progress (the thread's Draw). Because progress can be linear; arrival cannot.

**V-S46.** Intersection activation decides ambient liveness per segment. Because off-screen weather is a generator running in an empty field (V-M54).

**V-S47.** The runway's virtual length is style, not per-frame script measurement. Because the track is laid once; the train doesn't re-survey it (engineering inheritance).

**V-S48.** Wheel, touch, and keyboard produce identical journeys: Space/PageDown/arrows traverse lawfully. Because parity across inputs is the story's promise (XR14/V-I-IV).

**V-S49.** Scroll restoration is sessional: fresh visits start at the threshold, greeted not resumed. Because position memory is not story memory (SPEC-005 §11/V-S35).

**V-S50.** The reduced-motion scroll presents instant section states with opacity-only transitions. Because the stills telling turns pages, gracefully (V-M73).

**V-S51.** Pinned scenes use the runway's own holds; CSS sticky where it suffices; no new scroll libraries. Because every scroll primitive imports someone else's physics (V-M89/§19).

**V-S52.** No momentum-trap custom scrollers, no wheel-overriding "smooth scroll". Because N26 is absolute and twice-written (V-M89/S2).

**V-S53.** iOS rubber-banding never tears masks: overscroll contained at the body. Because the phone's rubber band is the phone's; our masks are ours.

**V-S54.** Scroll-linked filters are banned; atmosphere moves as opacity and transform only. Because blur on scroll is a bonfire of frame budgets (§19.3).

**V-S55.** DPR-scaled canvas ambient pauses when its segment leaves view. Because pixel-rain offscreen is still rain you paid for (V-M54's canvas twin).

### V-S-V. Locks, doors, and earned passages (56–70)

**V-S56.** A locked segment resists with settle-back plus one calm line. Because the world explains the lock in its own voice, and the UI never scolds (V-M66/S37).

**V-S57.** Unlocking is a press act, never a scroll threshold crossed by accident. Because irreversible acts require intent, not momentum (decision-lock law).

**V-S58.** Anchors render from the store (`<chapter>:<moment>`); the skin invents no parallel state. Because one memory, one truth (REUSABLES anchor law).

**V-S59.** Door beats stage three visual states — approach-warmth, invite, commit — all ≥44px. Because the door is the site's sacred interaction and it is rehearsed, not improvised (R-68/S66).

**V-S60.** The ember door warms on approach and, on press, plays the closing beat — and never opens into a form. Because Ω holds: no crack, no seam, no funnel (N91/N92/S67).

**V-S61.** The Express lamp is lit from ~6s and never blinks into view. Because its presence releases pressure; its flash would create it (R-08/S8).

**V-S62.** Earned content stays revealed within its segment, including on upward scroll. Because take-backs punish the curious (earned law).

**V-S63.** Scroll position never becomes a statistic ("you've seen 73%"). Because self-tracking chrome is a pedometer on a poem (V-S32's named instance).

**V-S64.** Crossing a trigger commits its migration to completion. Because half-chapters on release is the world stuck between two weathers (V-S7/V-C8).

**V-S65.** During the story's three Holds (cost-pause, suspense, destination line), scroll input is absorbed, not fought. Because a held breath shouldn't sputter (S53/S55/S65-family).

**V-S66.** The fade to black is gated to its beat; afterward the signature holds without any scroll cue. Because the last stages are watched, not steered (S69/FU.8).

**V-S67.** Post-curtain, the runway is over: the real-door column scrolls plainly. Because normalcy after the signature is the generosity (V-L95).

**V-S68.** No back-to-top furniture. Because the journey has doors, not elevators (§5.4/L44-family).

**V-S69.** Deep links land at chapter vistas with the vista ceremony intact. Because a slug is a permanent promise (NR-14, when traversal ships; recorded).

**V-S70.** The scrollbar stays native and unskinned. Because the browser's handshake is the visitor's, not ours to redecorate.

### V-S-VI. Dignity and QA (71–85)

**V-S71.** Exit-permission exists at every border: the straight line is offered and calm. Because the fast tier completes everywhere or the covenant is broken (XR32/T7).

**V-S72.** No retention screens, no "are you sure", no scroll-blocks. Because the door out is half the trust of the door in (S93/XR8).

**V-S73.** The four-gait walkthrough — fast flick, slow read, keyboard-only, touch-only — runs per scene. Because the story is one story under four hands (§18.3/§20 rule 18).

**V-S74.** Jank audit runs on the slowest canonical device: atmosphere degrades before rhythm. Because the aisle seat is the courtroom (ER-81/V-M93/M94).

**V-S75.** Runway heights audit per segment: no orphaned dead-scroll pixels. Because dead scroll is a false floor, and false floors are lies (V-L89's vertical twin).

**V-S76.** Every scroll-driven act ships its reduced/stills twin, verified. Because parity is proven, not presumed (§18.3).

**V-S77.** Beats fire at identical positions across browsers: no engine-specific pixel math. Because a story that shifts per browser is a rumor, not a telling.

**V-S78.** Scroll during font swap settles without reflow drama. Because the pipeline promised invisible swap on locked lines (V-T90/T93).

**V-S79.** Memory audit: long sessions grow no listeners. Because dispose discipline is respect for the tenth minute as much as the first (V-M99).

**V-S80.** Battery: a 10-minute parked session is near-silent. Because the machine idles like a good engine — barely (V-M98).

**V-S81.** Back/forward cache restores the sessional-fresh state: no ghost-resume. Because bfcache is a second first impression (V-S49's browser twin).

**V-S82.** Migrations hold contrast continuity through every sampled frame of scroll. Because the crossing must be safe at every centimeter (V-C51/V-C94).

**V-S83.** No beat requires pixel-perfect scrolling to discover. Because discovery is authored; pixel-hunting is QA by the visitor (N23's scroll echo).

**V-S84.** The visitor can always stop and breathe: nothing punishes stillness. Because idle is data, not failure (S85).

**V-S85.** Time estimates shown anywhere hold their promise. Because a broken "2 min" is a lie told in seconds (S90).

### V-S-VII. Final scroll laws (86–100)

**V-S86.** One scroll = one act: no nested scroll regions inside the runway. Because two wheels in one hand is a fight the hand loses.

**V-S87.** The visitor's velocity is information, never a target to correct. Because pace is the visitor's sovereignty made measurable (V-S19's dignity twin).

**V-S88.** Scroll-linked type never outruns legibility at the fastest lawful flick. Because scrubbed words that smear are words that hide (V-M34's scroll case).

**V-S89.** Migrations never fire on momentum alone: position-gated, velocity-informed. Because the weather changes where you are, not how fast you ran past (V-S17).

**V-S90.** A chapter crossing is felt as arrival — deceleration honored to the end. Because teleportation wastes the agency the scroll purchased (T2-family/S87).

**V-S91.** The ocean test: any ten-second random scroll excerpt still reads as the story mid-breath. Because states beautiful at rest are states beautiful mid-scroll (V-M2's motion corollary).

**V-S92.** Scroll-depth is not instrumented: no analytics on the hand. Because the rooms are the visitor's (N86/§19 telemetry law).

**V-S93.** The runway's end is honest: the signature is the last beat; nothing scrolls after meaning ends. Because encore-scroll is encore-bait in disguise (N93/N96).

**V-S94.** Motion settings are reachable without a journey's worth of scrolling. Because consent furniture must be closer than the thing it governs (ER-82/V-L67).

**V-S95.** All scroll assumptions hold under 200% zoom. Because the runway transposes like the type (V-L77's scroll case).

**V-S96.** The four tellings ship their scroll behaviors together: cinematic scrub, stills paging, express linear, semantic order — one story. Because parity ships whole or not at all (S94).

**V-S97.** Scroll is reviewed as choreography: every beat, hold, and air named in the scene manifest. Because unnamed scroll is unowned scroll (V-M95's track echo).

**V-S98.** Assistive-tech traversal order matches narrative order at every pixel of the runway. Because the Semantic telling is a telling, not a transcript of a different story (V-I50).

**V-S99.** No new scroll primitive ships without updating this register's QA rows. Because the vocabulary grows by amendment (PART II charter/V-L99 twin).

**V-S100.** Above all: the hand believes; the story delivers — every pixel of scroll answers worthily. Because the scroll is the promise the whole site makes, repeated (S100's kinetic form).

## REGISTER V-I — 100 INTERACTION RULES

_Every touch answered calmly, instantly, honestly. Grammar, hover, press, focus, cursor, the machine's voice, dignity._

### V-I-I. The grammar (1–10)

**V-I1.** Hover reveals hidden context; press expands reasoning or commits; scroll progresses thinking. Because the triad is locked and it is enough (REUSABLES interaction laws/§0.3).

**V-I2.** One offered action per beat. Because the least-oriented moment deserves Hick's mercy (S6).

**V-I3.** Every input maps to one predictable consequence. Because surprise-behavior breeds caution, and caution stops touching (S92).

**V-I4.** A control ships only with its consequence working. Because dead affordances are broken promises with styling (consequence law, SPEC-005 §8).

**V-I5.** Reactions answer in under 100ms; acknowledgment within the react band (90ms). Because "it noticed me" dies in queue (S4/ER-3).

**V-I6.** Nothing the visitor didn't move moves first. Because autonomy is the premise's foundation (S91/C21).

**V-I7.** Affordances are honest: doors look openable, reading looks readable. Because un-taught interaction fails as dead-world or slot-machine (N23/V-L90 twin).

**V-I8.** Interactive states are five, named and styled: hover, focus-visible, active, locked/disabled, earned. Because unnamed states ship unstyled, and unstyled states ship confusion.

**V-I9.** The world reacts to gestures, never to dossiers. Because personalization theater is surveillance in a concierge uniform (S95).

**V-I10.** Fake reactivity is banned: whatever responds, responds truly. Because one canned sequence retroactively un-feels every prior delight (N18).

### V-I-II. Hover (11–25)

**V-I11.** Hover previews; it never commits. Because previewing is context; committing is contract — different ceremonies.

**V-I12.** Hover reveals are additive: context appears; the stage never rearranges. Because layout shift on hover is the floor moving while you read the sign (no-CLS interaction law).

**V-I13.** Hover on labels: accent text-tier plus the underline Draw — the thread comes to meet you. Because the house gesture should be felt before the first click (V-M24).

**V-I14.** Hover glow on door beats stays inside glow-tier ration. Because warmth is rationed like the accent that supplies it (V-C4/C57).

**V-I15.** Hover curves: 90–200ms in, 200–420ms out. Because leaving should be calmer than arriving — the world's goodbye is never abrupt (S87 family).

**V-I16.** Un-hover settles fully: no sticky half-reveals. Because a half-open door reads as a broken door.

**V-I17.** Hover-in acknowledges under 100ms even when the full reveal takes its token time. Because acknowledgment is the delight; the reveal is the content (V-I5).

**V-I18.** Every hover reveal exists identically on focus-within. Because the keyboard is a hand too (XR14/V-I52).

**V-I19.** Hover is never required: every reveal has a parity path. Because hover-only content is content locked behind a pointing device (§18.2).

**V-I20.** Coarse pointers receive the tap-mapped equivalent: first contact previews, second commits — where lawful. Because touch has hover's intent split into two taps (V-I25's policy).

**V-I21.** Hover-neighbor invites (earned-label law) stay within one radial relationship: no chain light-ups. Because the world leans toward attention; it doesn't chase it (REUSABLES earned-label law).

**V-I22.** Hover never triggers sound by itself. Because audio needs the story's own act, not a coincidence of cursor (T4/consent law).

**V-I23.** Hover targets are ≥44px including invisible padding. Because the law of touch geometry applies to the pointing hand too (§11.4).

**V-I24.** Hover on text uses color and underline — never scale. Because text that grows under the cursor is text that dodges the reader.

**V-I25.** The world answers presence <100ms but never chases the cursor beyond lawful reach. Because a world that follows too eagerly is furniture that barks (V-I21's physics twin).

### V-I-III. Press and commit (26–40)

**V-I26.** Press is the contracting act: expand reasoning (the dialog) or commit a decision — both consequential, never casual. Because click = expand reasoning / press = the story's gravity (interaction laws).

**V-I27.** The decision commit is irreversible by story law, staged inside the 1750ms suspense and total stillness. Because "no perfect answer" must be felt bodily before it lands (S55/V-M62/anchor-as-lock law).

**V-I28.** Press feedback begins ≤90ms, before the beat performs. Because state change first, ceremony second — the hand must own the moment (V-I5).

**V-I29.** Enter and Space perform identically to pointer press, everywhere. Because one act has many hands (S5/XR14).

**V-I30.** Focus order follows narrative order; nothing interactive hides off-order. Because order is meaning's third telling (V-I44/L71).

**V-I31.** ESC closes the reasoning dialog; focus returns to the invoker. Because exit from a thought should be as graceful as entry (dialog laws).

**V-I32.** Dialogs are one at a time, dismissible, never stacked. Because stacked interruption is a pile-up of neglects (N25/XR21).

**V-I33.** Commit acts show consequence in-world before any confirmation furniture. Because the world responds first; chrome never speaks for it (consequence-first law).

**V-I34.** Double-activation on a commit is idempotent. Because fast fingers are not a bug in the visitor.

**V-I35.** Locked controls respond to press with settle-back plus one line. Because the world explains its locks; the UI never says "no" in chrome (V-S56/I72 echo).

**V-I36.** No destructive acts exist: nothing deletes visitor progress or earned anchors. Because the story keeps its promises literally, in storage (journey-store law).

**V-I37.** Primary acts are ≥48px on coarse pointers; all acts ≥44px. Because thumbs are honest instruments and deserve honest targets (§11.4).

**V-I38.** Active state is pressed-in — weight or darken within the ramp — never a foreign color pop. Because feedback should feel like physics, not confetti (V-C58/V-C61).

**V-I39.** The press that unlocks a segment plays its unlock solo once; replay is calm. Because earned states don't need fireworks re-run (earned law/V-S62).

**V-I40.** Every press beat has a still twin in the reduced telling: instant result, zero ceremony lost. Because parity is cast from the same script (S94/V-M71).

### V-I-IV. Focus and keyboard (41–55)

**V-I41.** A visible focus ring on every interactive element: chapter-accented, ≥3:1, 2px, 2px offset. Because orientation for keyboard visitors is a right, styled with respect (§14 law/V-C49).

**V-I42.** Focus is never clipped by masks: mask containers allow focus overflow on interactive children. Because a focus ring cut in half is a promise cut in half.

**V-I43.** The skip link is first in tab order. Because the story's own readers deserve the straight line too (§14 law).

**V-I44.** Tab order = narrative order = DOM order. Because one story, three facts, zero surprises (V-I30/V-L71).

**V-I45.** Keyboard covers the full inventory per scene: doors, stations, commits, dialog, furniture. Because parity is a checklist, not a sentiment (XR14).

**V-I46.** Keyboard scrolling of the runway behaves: focus follows content into view. Because a focus ring off-screen is a guide dog on the wrong floor (§18.2 matrix).

**V-I47.** Roving tabindex appears only where a true composite demands it; default is natural order. Because cleverness in tab order is confusion in a trench coat.

**V-I48.** aria-hidden mirrors visual gating exactly. Because sighted pacing and AT pacing must share one script (Gated law).

**V-I49.** The single aria-live voice announces beats without chatter. Because one voice, no noise (REUSABLES one-voice law).

**V-I50.** The Semantic telling carries identical copy and beat order. Because a screen-reader visitor receives the argument, not a pamphlet about it (V-M78).

**V-I51.** Focus styles never rely on color alone: shape and offset twin. Because meaning survives grayscale (V-C10's focus case).

**V-I52.** Every hover-reveal is reachable by focus. Because the keyboard is a hand (V-I18's rule form).

**V-I53.** No keyboard traps, anywhere, ever. Because a trap is a broken promise to a hand that trusted us (N27).

**V-I54.** Reduced-motion focus movement is instant — no smooth-scroll chasing. Because the reduced telling doesn't perform navigation (V-M72 family).

**V-I55.** Landmarks stay where AT expects: banner, main, contentinfo. Because furniture has addresses in the readers' maps (§14 law).

### V-I-V. Cursor and presence (56–70)

**V-I56.** The custom cursor is a reading light: cosmetic, minimal, never a functional requirement. Because attention made visible must never become attention made mandatory (§16.1).

**V-I57.** The halo is a soft radial field ≤240px diameter, ≤0.35 alpha, chapter-tinted. Because fog lifts where attention lands — gently, or it startles (§16.1/§17.2).

**V-I58.** Cursor states are four, named: read, door, press, hold. Because unnamed cursor states become cursor mood swings (§16.2).

**V-I59.** Cursor morphs acknowledge ≤90ms. Because the hand reads lag as brokenness (V-I5's cursor case).

**V-I60.** Magnetic pull ≤6px, instant settle, never on text. Because a door may lean toward the hand; a sentence may not (V-M86/V-I24).

**V-I61.** Cursor trails are banned. Because drag is comedy and presence is not residue (V-M85).

**V-I62.** Cursor visuals meet contrast on both dark and light fields. Because a light you can't see lights nothing (V-C82 family).

**V-I63.** The halo lifts fog and atmosphere only; it never dims content. Because the reading light illuminates; it doesn't spotlight (V-I57's boundary).

**V-I64.** Coarse pointers get no cursor layer: presence arrives through touch physics. Because a fake cursor on glass is a puppet (§16.4).

**V-I65.** Reduced motion: the halo is a static soft disc or off, per setting. Because the reading light respects the same telling (§16.4).

**V-I66.** The native cursor never hides without its exact replacement present; hit-testing lives on the element. Because a click that misses the real target is a door painted on a wall (§16.3).

**V-I67.** The halo dims during solos and holds. Because one voice performs; even the light holds its breath (conductor law, cursor case).

**V-I68.** Presence data never leaves the machine. Because attention is the visitor's property (S95/V-I9 technical twin).

**V-I69.** The custom cursor vanishes the instant the platform would: window exit, touch begin. Because a light left burning in an empty room is a bug, not a mood.

**V-I70.** Presence-reactive ambience answers <100ms even when its full bloom is slower. Because acknowledgment precedes content (V-I17's ambient case).

### V-I-VI. Doors, hints, and the machine's voice (71–85)

**V-I71.** The door warms on approach within glow ration; the never-opening doctrine holds — no crack, no seam, no tease. Because Ω is the ending's entire reality (S67/R-68/N91).

**V-I72.** Hints escalate ambient → suggestive → direct in margin lines, in the world's voice — never modals, never mascots, never chat. Because stuckness is the story's failure, not the visitor's, and floor-staff leans rather than pokes (S37/R-34).

**V-I73.** Idle is data: progression offers on idle are gentle and ignorable. Because an idle visitor is asked, never grabbed (S85).

**V-I74.** The reasoning dialog keeps its laws: kicker, one idea, calm close, no stacking. Because thinking expands into stillness, not into windows (ThoughtCard law).

**V-I75.** Settings furniture is honest: plain words, lawful states, lower-half on mobile. Because consent machinery should read like a light switch, not a cockpit (§11.4/ER-82).

**V-I76.** Audio consent remains the story's own gesture; toggles are plain, remembered, never re-asked. Because silence is the default soundtrack and asking twice is pestering (T4 doctrine).

**V-I77.** Errors are courteous, specific, and blameless: ink, one sentence, one next step. Because the story's failures speak like their author (N79/A49).

**V-I78.** Empty states are serene. Because the story has no dead ends, so its empty states have nothing to apologize for (N27's positive form).

**V-I79.** Confirmation is consequence-first; after-the-fact toasts are banned. Because the world shows; chrome doesn't cheer (V-I33/N52 boundary).

**V-I80.** The store receives anchors and depth only; the skin collects nothing else. Because consent-first state is the whole data ethics (SPEC-005 §11/N89).

**V-I81.** Earned states render from anchors: visited reads visibly earned, with no take-backs. Because memory is a promise, and promises don't flicker (V-S58/S62).

**V-I82.** The Express Lane stays one gesture away, permanently lit. Because the straight line is respect, always visible, never blinking (T7/V-S61 echo).

**V-I83.** Inside the fiction's final ten seconds nothing is asked for: the real door lives post-signature as calm text. Because pressure at the curtain is the one unforgivable timing (S70/R-71/N92).

**V-I84.** The final beat has one voice: nothing interactive competes with the signature. Because the last stage holds a name, and the name holds alone (S69/V-T39).

**V-I85.** Hints never name the plot in advance. Because an affordance that spoils is a butler handing out the ending (narrative honesty of chrome).

### V-I-VII. Restraint and dignity (86–100)

**V-I86.** No modals, overlays, interstitials, or "before you go" furniture. Because interruption is felt aggression, everywhere, always (N25/XR21).

**V-I87.** No gamified feedback: points, streaks, badges, confetti, progress-currency. Because the story's rewards are comprehension and calm — worthless if extrinsic (N83).

**V-I88.** Exit is honored instantly from anywhere, without a single retention screen. Because the door out is half the trust of the door in (S93).

**V-I89.** No dark patterns of any species. Because the trust economy prices honesty and one pattern spends the bank (N30/XR20).

**V-I90.** No personalization by assumption: no role-greeting, no visit-count theater. Because the story reacts to gestures, never to dossiers (S95/N29).

**V-I91.** Errors are never the visitor's fault — in copy or in color. Because blame is the surest way to lose a reader mid-sentence (N79).

**V-I92.** Mouse, touch, pen, keyboard, and assistive tech are first-class citizens on every beat. Because bodies vary; the welcome doesn't (XR14/S94).

**V-I93.** Touch geometry is law: ≥44px targets, ≥8px separation, neighbor forgiveness. Because precision is luck in a moving aisle (§11.4/V-I37).

**V-I94.** Double-tap zoom conflicts are designed away from door beats. Because a door that fights the browser is a door that loses.

**V-I95.** Every interaction has a parity path requiring zero precision gestures. Because the welcome extends to every hand (§18.2/I19).

**V-I96.** Interaction QA runs the four gaits plus AT per scene. Because proof beats sentiment (§18.3/V-S73 twin).

**V-I97.** Interaction states are token-driven: durations and colors from tokens only. Because one number, one place — down to the last hover (SPEC-005 §20).

**V-I98.** A new interaction primitive requires a manifest entry and a parity plan before build. Because the grammar grows by amendment, never improvisation (PART II charter/V-I1).

**V-I99.** Every act is scored H/C/E/A/X at review. Because interaction is narrative before it is chrome (V-M70's final application).

**V-I100.** Above all, every touch is answered like the story answers: calmly, instantly, honestly. Because the medium is the interview, and the interview never pauses (S100 in the hand).
